# Data Mining on Unbalanced Data: Detection of Fraudulent Transactions on Unbalanced Datasets

**Author:** Niloofar Asadi  
**Research Type:** Unpublished Undergraduate Research Manuscript  
**Status:** Unpublished; not submitted to a journal or conference.

## Overview

This undergraduate research manuscript explores the detection of fraudulent transactions in imbalanced datasets, where fraudulent transactions represent a much smaller proportion of the data than legitimate transactions.

The research examines sampling strategies and machine-learning approaches to address class imbalance and improve fraud detection.

## Methods and Approaches

The manuscript discusses:

- Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE)
- Sampling strategies for imbalanced datasets
- k-Nearest Neighbours (k-NN)
- Decision Trees
- Neural Networks
- Support Vector Machines (SVM)
- Naive Bayes
